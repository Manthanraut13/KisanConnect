## Brand & Style
This design system governs an enterprise-grade agricultural supply chain and commerce infrastructure. It balances operational precision with the grounded reality of physical agriculture, bulk commodity trading, freight tracking, and cooperative procurement. 

The aesthetic is Modern Functionalist with a Tactile Cold-Light posture: cool, high-visibility surfaces paired with warm, organic operational accents. The user interface serves farm operators, grain elevator managers, global freight coordinators, and procurement brokers. It evokes total reliability, inventory transparency, physical custody clarity, and decisive speed without resorting to generic industrial blues or decorative clutter.

## Colors
The palette adopts a structured cold-light foundation offset by high-visibility earth and telemetry accents. Purple, dark navy, and synthetic indigo tones are strictly prohibited to prevent visual ambiguity with standard transport status codes.

### Base & Surface Tones
- **Base Background:** `#F0F4F8` (Mist cool off-white for wide workspace viewports)
- **Card Surfaces:** `#FAFCFD` (Flat structural content layers)
- **Elevated Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` (Modals, popovers, active drawers, and drag overlays)

### Accent & Functional Palette
- **Primary Accent (Sage-Teal):** `#2A9D8F` — Used for primary commitments, active inventory selections, verified status, and dominant actions.
- **Secondary Accent (Terracotta Amber):** `#E07A3B` — Used for transaction alerts, pending contracts, warm physical-harvest accents, and secondary focus.
- **Logistics (Slate-Teal):** `#3A7CA5` — Dedicated to waybills, transport legs, vessel tracking, and telematics nodes.
- **Admin & Infrastructure (Charcoal):** `#2D3E50` — Reserved for technical system metadata, ledger verifications, and audit states.
- **Success (Sage Green):** `#52B788` — Quality inspection approvals, payment settlements, and completed route legs.
- **Error (Brick Red):** `#C1440E` — Cargo spoilage warnings, quarantine alerts, failed inspections, and contract breaches.

### Neutrals & Boundaries
- **Neutral Primary:** `#1C2B3A` — High-contrast text, metric values, and primary structural headers.
- **Neutral Secondary:** `#4A6274` — Field labels, table headers, supporting commentary, and passive iconography.
- **Neutral Muted:** `#8FA3B1` — Placeholders, non-interactive timestamps, and deactivated states.
- **Border Neutral:** `#D5E1E8` — Subtle, high-legibility container outlines across `#F0F4F8`.

## Typography
The system uses Google Sans across all visual hierarchy levels to enforce clean industrial clarity and legibility across sunlight-exposed handheld terminals and multi-monitor operations desks.

- Use `weight: 800` strictly for high-impact commercial totals and macro operational dashboards (`display-lg`).
- Tabular figures must be enabled for all numerical ledgers, tonnage counts, yield percentages, and pricing tables via `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1`.
- For status tags, batch IDs, and commodity grade codes, use uppercase `label-sm` with `0.04em` letter spacing to guarantee scanability under rapid verification cycles.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout leverages a responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop and tablet viewports, collapsing to a single-column or dual-stack layout on mobile.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters (`space-lg`), 40px margins (`margin-desktop`). Heavy operations screens expand to a full-bleed layout capped at a maximum inner container width of 1600px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters (`gutter-tablet`), 32px margins (`margin-tablet`). Logistics routes and bill-of-lading panels collapse into vertical split views.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters (`gutter-mobile`), 16px margins (`margin-mobile`). All tabular datasets collapse into stacked operational cards.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Built upon an 8px spatial grid, utilizing 4px (`space-2xs`) micro-increments strictly for dense data rows, pill badges, and input padding.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created by pairing soft, cool borders with ambient tinted shadow structures to prevent visual weight buildup in data-heavy screens.

- **Flat Container Level:** Standard cards sit flush on `#F0F4F8` using `#FAFCFD` fill and a single `1px solid #D5E1E8` boundary without shadows.
- **Hover & Active Depth:** When cards, manifests, or grid rows are hovered or targeted, apply the system ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 12px rgba(28, 43, 58, 0.07)`. Surface remains `#FAFCFD` with border shifting to `#4A6274`.
- **Floating Overlays & Modals:** Elevated containers, sliding freight sheets, and contextual menus use background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D5E1E8`, and stacked elevation: `box-shadow: 0 2px 12px rgba(28, 43, 58, 0.07), 0 12px 32px rgba(28, 43, 58, 0.08)`.
- No black or uncolored shadows are permitted; all drop shadows must utilize alpha-blends of Neutral Primary (`#1C2B3A`).

## Shapes
Geometry is strictly governed to distinguish functional roles: structural containers employ stable, sweeping curves, interactive inputs remain sharp and ergonomic, and status badges take full pill curvature.

- **Cards & Data Modules:** Radii must be fixed at `16px` (`rounded-lg` level).
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** Radii must be fixed at `10px` for text fields, dropdown selectors, numeric adjusters, and date pickers.
- **Pills, Badges & Chips:** Radii must be fixed at `20px` to create distinct status units.
- **Action Buttons:** Standard action triggers inherit `10px` to match companion input fields; segmented view switchers utilize `10px`.

- **Logistics Waypoint Stepper:** Linear track with `#D5E1E8` progress lines, completed nodes styled in `#52B788`, current vehicle/vessel node in `#3A7CA5` with ambient pulse shadow, and pending waypoints in `#8FA3B1`.
