# Kisan Connect User-Facing Knowledge Base

## 1. Platform Overview & Key Features
Kisan Connect is an AI-powered direct farm-to-consumer digital marketplace in India that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, and FPOs.

- **Direct Farm-to-Consumer Sales**: Eliminates middlemen commission fees, giving farmers fair revenue and consumers fresh produce at competitive rates.
- **Secure Escrow Payments**: Buyer payments are safely held in Kisan Connect Escrow and released to the farmer's bank account upon verified delivery.
- **Real-Time Order & Logistics Tracking**: Live tracking of orders across status states: Pending -> Confirmed -> Picked Up -> In Transit -> Delivered. View delivery driver details and ETA.
- **24/7 Grievance & Dispute Support**: Instant support ticket registration for produce quality issues or delivery delays with 24-hour resolution guarantee.
- **FPO (Farmer Producer Organization) Hub**: Enables smallholder farmers to pool produce for bulk sales to institutional buyers and supermarkets.

## 2. User-Facing AI Features
- **Dynamic Price Recommendation**: Suggests optimal selling prices (in ₹/kg) based on historical Agmarknet mandi benchmarks and real-time market demand.
- **7-Day AI Demand Forecasting**: Predicts future demand spikes for crops (e.g., Tomato, Onion, Potato, Wheat) to help farmers plan harvest and listing dates.
- **Smart Route Optimization**: Uses Dijkstra and TSP algorithms to optimize delivery routes for logistics partners, reducing transit time and produce spoilage.

## 3. How-To User Guides

### How Farmers List & Sell Crops
1. Log in to Kisan Connect with your registered mobile number and OTP.
2. Go to your **Farmer Dashboard** and click **"Add New Listing"**.
3. Select Commodity (e.g., Tomato, Onion, Potato, Wheat), Grade, and Quantity (in kg).
4. Enter your selling price per kg or click **"Use AI Price Recommendation"**.
5. Upload clear photos of your harvest and click **"Publish Listing"**.
6. When a buyer orders, you will receive an SMS and app notification. Hand over produce to the assigned Kisan Connect logistics driver.

### How Consumers Buy Fresh Produce & Track Orders
1. Log in to Kisan Connect as a Consumer.
2. Browse fresh crops on the Marketplace or search by commodity/district.
3. Add items to your cart, select your delivery address, and pay securely via UPI, Card, or NetBanking.
4. Go to **"My Orders"** to track live delivery status, view vehicle driver contact info, and see estimated arrival time.
5. Provide delivery verification OTP to the driver upon arrival and rate your purchase.

### How Bulk Buyers & FPOs Operate
- **Bulk Buyers**: Select "Bulk Order", input required crop volume (e.g., 500 kg+), request custom price quotes, and lock in direct farmer/FPO contracts.
- **FPOs**: Register cooperative members, aggregate crop listings into pooled inventory, and manage group payouts directly through the FPO dashboard.

### Order Cancellation & Refund Policy
- Orders can be cancelled free of cost before logistics pickup.
- If produce is damaged upon arrival, report it via Kisan Mitra Chatbot or the Grievances tab within 12 hours for immediate refund or replacement.
